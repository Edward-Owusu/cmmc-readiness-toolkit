# Methodology

## Scope

This version covers **CMMC Level 1**, which protects Federal Contract Information (FCI). Level 1 consists of the 15 basic safeguarding requirements in FAR 52.204-21(b)(1), as adopted in the CMMC program rule (32 CFR Part 170). Level 2 (the 110 requirements of NIST SP 800-171) and Level 3 are not yet covered.

## Questions

Each of the 15 practices has one to three plain-language questions, 30 in total. The questions were written for this tool to make each requirement understandable to a non-specialist; they are not the official assessment objectives. Each question also names the evidence a company should keep, such as a user list, a visitor log, or update settings.

Answers are **Yes**, **Partly**, **No**, or **N/A**.

## Practice status

| Status | Rule |
|---|---|
| MET | Every question is answered Yes, or N/A with a written justification |
| NOT MET | Any question is answered No or Partly, or N/A without a justification |
| INCOMPLETE | No question fails, but at least one is unanswered |

"Partly" counts as not met, because a requirement that is applied to only some systems is not met.

## Readiness

The organization is shown as ready for the annual Level 1 self-assessment only when **all 15 practices are met**. Under the CMMC program rule, a Level 1 self-assessment must find every requirement met; plans of action and milestones are not permitted at Level 1. Level 1 results and an affirmation by a senior company official are submitted annually through the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS).

## Action plan

Unmet practices are listed with the specific questions that failed and a recommended fix. Each practice is tagged as a **Quick win** (usually achievable with settings, simple procedures, or records) or a **Project** (usually needing new equipment, network changes, or new services). Quick wins are listed first so a small company can make visible progress quickly.

## System Security Plan draft

The tool assembles a draft System Security Plan from the company profile and answers: system identification, the boundary of systems handling FCI, and for each practice its status, implementation description (taken from notes on questions answered Yes), evidence, N/A justifications, gaps, and planned actions. A System Security Plan is a formal requirement at Level 2; at Level 1 it is good practice that supports the self-assessment and prepares a company for Level 2.

## Limitations

- The results reflect the answers given; the tool does not inspect systems.
- It is not a CMMC assessment, certification, or legal advice. Confirm current requirements in 32 CFR Part 170, your contract, and official DoD CMMC guidance.
