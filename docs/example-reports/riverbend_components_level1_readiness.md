## Action plan (quick wins first)

1. **AC.L1-B.1.II Transaction and Function Control** (Quick win, not met)
   - AC2-1: Not in place. Have you defined what each type of user is allowed to do (for example, only administrators can install software or change settings)?
   - AC2-2: Not in place. Are everyday users prevented from having administrator rights on their computers?
   - Fix: Define user roles, remove administrator rights from everyday accounts, and give administrators a separate account for admin work.
2. **AC.L1-B.1.I Authorized Access Control** (Quick win, not met)
   - AC1-2: Only partly in place. Do you keep a list of the devices (computers, phones, servers) allowed to connect to those systems?
   - Fix: Create and maintain a list of authorized users and devices, turn off guest and anonymous access, and require a sign-in on every system that holds FCI.
3. **AC.L1-B.1.IV Control Public Information** (Quick win, not met)
   - AC4-2: Only partly in place. Is public content reviewed to make sure it contains no FCI, both before posting and periodically afterward?
   - Fix: Name who may post public content, require review for FCI before anything is published, and check existing public content periodically.
4. **IA.L1-B.1.V Identification** (Quick win, not met)
   - IA1-1: Not in place. Does every person have their own user account, with no shared logins?
   - Fix: Replace shared logins with individual accounts and record every device and service account with a unique name.
5. **IA.L1-B.1.VI Authentication** (Quick win, not met)
   - IA2-2: Only partly in place. Are default passwords on new devices, software, and equipment changed before they are used?
   - Fix: Require authentication on every system and change all default passwords; adding multi-factor authentication is strongly recommended and is required at Level 2.
6. **PE.L1-B.1.IX Manage Visitors and Physical Access** (Quick win, not met)
   - PE2-3: Only partly in place. Do you control keys, badges, and lock combinations: knowing who has them, collecting them when people leave, and changing combinations when needed?
   - Fix: Escort visitors, keep a sign-in log, and track who holds keys and badges, collecting them at departure.
7. **SI.L1-B.1.XII Flaw Remediation** (Quick win, not met)
   - SI1-2: Only partly in place. Do you install security updates for operating systems and software within a set time frame?
   - Fix: Turn on automatic updates where possible, subscribe to vendor security alerts, and set a time frame for installing critical updates.
8. **SI.L1-B.1.XIII Malicious Code Protection** (Quick win, not met)
   - SI2-1: Only partly in place. Is antivirus or endpoint protection installed on every computer and server?
   - Fix: Deploy endpoint protection on every computer and server, and turn on malware and phishing filtering for email.
9. **AC.L1-B.1.III External Connections** (Project, not met)
   - AC3-1: Not in place. Have you identified the outside systems people use for company work, such as personal devices, home computers, cloud services, and customer or partner portals?
   - AC3-2: Not in place. Do you control or limit those connections, for example by allowing company data only on approved devices and services?
   - Fix: List the external systems and services in use, approve the ones that are needed, and block or restrict company data on everything else.
10. **SC.L1-B.1.X Boundary Protection** (Project, not met)
   - SC1-2: Not in place. Is the firewall set to block unnecessary incoming traffic, and are its settings and logs reviewed?
   - Fix: Put a business-grade firewall at the internet edge, block all inbound traffic that is not needed, and review its rules and alerts regularly.
